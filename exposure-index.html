Laundering

Epistemic laundering is the concealment of human editorial choices behind the appearance of machine-generated objectivity. None of the twenty-five expert personas were given the phrase. Yet 163 responses across eight models generated it independently — a concept so embedded in the training data that it surfaces without invitation.
